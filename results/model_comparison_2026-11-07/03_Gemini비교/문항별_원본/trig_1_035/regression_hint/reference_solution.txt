[정답] ④

[해설]
$\cos \left( \frac{\pi}{2} - \theta \right) = \sin \theta$이므로
$2 \cos \theta + \sin \theta = 0$
$2 \cos \theta = - \sin \theta$				$\cdots$$\cdots$ ㉠
$4 \cos^{2} \theta = \sin^{2} \theta$
$\sin^{2} \theta + \cos^{2} \theta = 1$이므로
$4 \cos^{2} \theta = 1 - \cos^{2} \theta$
$\cos^{2} \theta = \frac{1}{5}$
$\sin \theta < 0$이므로
㉠에 의하여 $\cos \theta > 0$
따라서 $\cos \theta = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
