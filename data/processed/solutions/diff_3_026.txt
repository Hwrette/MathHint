$f (x)$$= (x - a) (x - a r) (x - a r^{2}) + 9$
$=$$x^{3} - a (1 + r + r^{2}) x^{2} + a^{2} r (1 + r + r^{2}) x - (a r)^{3} + 9$
$f' (x) =$$3 x^{2} - 2 a (1 + r + r^{2}) x + a^{2} r (1 + r + r^{2})$
$f (0) = - (a r)^{3} + 9 = 1$
$f' (2)$$= 12 - 4 a (1 + r + r^{2}) + a^{2} r (1 + r + r^{2})$
$= - 2$
$a r = 2$, $a (1 + r + r^{2}) = 7$
$\therefore$$f (3) = 27 - 63 + 42 + 1$$= 7$
