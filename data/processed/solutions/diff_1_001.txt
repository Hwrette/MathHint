[정답] $9$

[해설]
함수
$f \left( x \right) = \left\{\begin{array}{lll}- a x + 2 a &  & \left( x < 2 \right) \\ a x - 2 a &  & \left( x \ge 2 \right)\end{array}\right.$
에 대하여
(ⅰ) $k < 2$일 때
$\lim_{x \rightarrow k} \frac{f \left( x \right) - f \left( k \right)}{x - k}$$= \lim_{x \rightarrow k} \frac{- a x + 2 a - \left( - a k + 2 a \right)}{x - k}$
$= \lim_{x \rightarrow k} \frac{- a \left( x - k \right)}{x - k} = - a$
이므로
$\lim_{x \rightarrow k +} \frac{f \left( x \right) - f \left( k \right)}{x - k} - \lim_{x \rightarrow k -} \frac{f \left( x \right) - f \left( k \right)}{x - k}$
$= - a - \left( - a \right) = 0 \ne 6$
(ⅱ) $k = 2$일 때
$\lim_{x \rightarrow 2 +} \frac{f \left( x \right) - f \left( 2 \right)}{x - 2} - \lim_{x \rightarrow 2 -} \frac{f \left( x \right) - f \left( 2 \right)}{x - 2}$
$= \lim_{x \rightarrow 2 +} \frac{a x - 2 a}{x - 2} - \lim_{x \rightarrow 2 -} \frac{- a x + 2 a}{x - 2}$
$= a - \left( - a \right) = 2 a$
$2 a = 6$에서 $a = 3$
(ⅲ) $k > 2$일 때
$\lim_{x \rightarrow k} \frac{f \left( x \right) - f \left( k \right)}{x - k}$$= \lim_{x \rightarrow k} \frac{a x - 2 a - \left( a k - 2 a \right)}{x - k}$
$= \lim_{x \rightarrow k} \frac{a \left( x - k \right)}{x - k} = a$
이므로
$\lim_{x \rightarrow k +} \frac{f \left( x \right) - f \left( k \right)}{x - k} - \lim_{x \rightarrow k -} \frac{f \left( x \right) - f \left( k \right)}{x - k}$
$= a - a = 0 \ne 6$
(ⅰ), (ⅱ), (ⅲ)에 의하여 $a = 3$, $k = 2$이므로
$f \left( a + k \right) = f \left( 3 + 2 \right) = f \left( 5 \right) = 9$
