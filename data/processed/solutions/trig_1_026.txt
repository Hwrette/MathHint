[정답] ②

[해설]
$\cos \left( \pi + \theta \right) = - \cos \theta = \frac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5}$에서    $\cos \theta = - \frac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5}$
$\frac{\pi}{2} < \theta < \pi$이므로
$\sin \theta$$= \sqrt{1 - \cos^{2} \theta}$$= \sqrt{1 - \left( - \frac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5} \right)^{2}}$$= \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
$\therefore$ $\sin \theta + \cos \theta$$= \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} - \frac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5}$$= - \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
