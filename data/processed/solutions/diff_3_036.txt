[정답] $\mathrm{11}$

[해설]
$f' (x) = 3 x^{2} - 3 = 3 (x + 1) (x - 1)$이므로

$x$	$\cdots$	$- 1$	$\cdots$	$\mathrm{1}$	$\cdots$
$f' (x)$	$+$	$\mathrm{0}$	$-$	$\mathrm{0}$	$+$
$f (x)$	$\nearrow$	극대	$\searrow$	극소	$\nearrow$

$x = 1$에서 극솟값을 가지므로 $a = 1$
$f (1) = 1^{3} - 3 \times 1 + 12 = 10$
$\therefore \quad a + f (a) = 1 + 10 = 11$
