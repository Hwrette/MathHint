[문제]
$\sin \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right) + \cos \left( \frac{\pi}{2} - \theta \right) = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$일 때, $\sin \theta \cos \theta$의 값은?
① $- \frac{2}{5}$	② $- \frac{1}{5}$		③ $0$
④ $\frac{1}{5}$		⑤ $\frac{2}{5}$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ①

[해설]
$\sin \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right) = \cos \theta$, $\cos \left( \frac{\pi}{2} - \theta \right) = \sin \theta$이므로
$\cos \theta + \sin \theta = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
$\left( \cos \theta + \sin \theta \right)^{2}$$= \cos^{2} \theta + 2 \cos \theta \sin \theta + \sin^{2} \theta$
$= 1 + 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta = \frac{1}{5}$
따라서 $\sin \theta \cos \theta = - \frac{2}{5}$
