[문제]
$3 \sin^{2} \left( \theta + \frac{2}{3} \pi \right) = 8 \sin \left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$일 때, $\cos \left( \theta - \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$의 값은?
① $\frac{1}{6}$		② $\frac{1}{5}$		③ $\frac{1}{4}$
④ $\frac{1}{3}$		⑤ $\frac{1}{2}$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ④

[해설]
$\theta - \frac{\pi}{3} = \alpha$라 하면
$3 \sin^{2} \left( \theta + \frac{2}{3} \pi \right)$$= 3 \sin^{2} (\pi + \alpha)$
$= 3 \sin^{2} \alpha = 3 - 3 \cos^{2} \alpha$
$8 \sin \left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{6} \right) = 8 \sin \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \alpha \right) = 8 \cos \alpha$
이므로 $3 - 3 \cos^{2} \alpha = 8 \cos \alpha$에서
$3 \cos^{2} \alpha + 8 \cos \alpha - 3 = 0$
$\left( 3 \cos \alpha - 1 \right) (\cos \alpha + 3) = 0$
$- 1 \leq \cos \alpha \leq 1$이므로    $\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{3}$
따라서    $\cos \left( \theta - \frac{\pi}{3} \right) = \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{3}$
