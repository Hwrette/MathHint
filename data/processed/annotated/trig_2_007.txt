[문제]
$\frac{\pi}{2} < \theta < \pi$인 $\theta$에 대하여 $\sin^{4} \theta + \cos^{4} \theta = \frac{23}{32}$일 때, $\sin \theta - \cos \theta$의 값은?
① $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$	② $1$		③ $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$
④ $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$	⑤ $\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ⑤

[해설]
$\sin^{4} \theta + \cos^{4} \theta$$= \left( \sin^{2} \theta + \cos^{2} \theta \right)^{2} - 2 \sin^{2} \theta \cos^{2} \theta$
$= 1 - 2 \sin^{2} \theta \cos^{2} \theta$$= \frac{23}{32}$
에서 $\sin^{2} \theta \cos^{2} \theta = \frac{9}{64}$
$\frac{\pi}{2} < \theta < \pi$에서 $\sin \theta > 0$, $\cos \theta < 0$이므로
$\sin \theta \cos \theta = - \frac{3}{8}$
$\left( \sin \theta - \cos \theta \right)^{2} = 1 - 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta = \frac{7}{4}$
따라서 $\sin \theta - \cos \theta = \frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}$
