[문제]
다항함수 $f (x)$에 대하여 $\lim_{h \rightarrow 0} \frac{f (1 + h) - f (1 - h)}{h} = 6$일 때, $\lim_{x \rightarrow 1} \frac{f \left( x^{3} \right) - f (1)}{x - 1}$의 값은?
① $\mathrm{9}$		② $\mathrm{11}$		③ $\mathrm{13}$
④ $\mathrm{15}$		⑤ $\mathrm{17}$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ①

[해설]
다항함수 $f (x)$는 $x = 1$에서 미분가능하므로
$6$$= \lim_{h \rightarrow 0} \frac{f \left( 1 + h \right) - f \left( 1 - h \right)}{h}$
$= \lim_{h \rightarrow 0} \left\{ \frac{f (1 + h) - f (1)}{h} + \frac{f (1 - h) - f (1)}{- h} \right\} = 2 f' (1)$
에서 $f' (1) = 3$
$\lim_{x \rightarrow 1} \frac{f \left( x^{3} \right) - f (1)}{x - 1}$$= \lim_{x \rightarrow 1} \left\{ \frac{f \left( x^{3} \right) - f (1)}{x^{3} - 1} \times \left( x^{2} + x + 1 \right) \right\}$
$= f' (1) \times 3 = 9$
