[문제]
$\cos \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right) = - \frac{1}{5}$일 때, $\frac{\sin \theta}{1 - \cos^{2} \theta}$의 값은?
① $\mathrm{- 5}$		② $- \sqrt{5}$	③ $0$
④ $\sqrt{5}$		⑤ $5$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ⑤

[해설]
$\cos \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right) = - \sin \theta = - \frac{1}{5}$에서    $\sin \theta = \frac{1}{5}$
$1 - \cos^{2} \theta = \sin^{2} \theta = \frac{1}{25}$
$\therefore$ $\frac{\sin \theta}{1 - \cos^{2} \theta}$$= 5$
