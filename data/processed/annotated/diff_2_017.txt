[문제]
함수
$f \left( x \right) = {\left\{\begin{array}{lll}3 x + a &  & \left( x \leq 1 \right) \\ 2 x^{3} + b x + 1 &  & \left( x > 1 \right)\end{array}\right.}$
이 $x = 1$에서 미분가능할 때, $a + b$의 값은? (단, $a$, $b$는 상수이다.)
① $\mathrm{- 8}$		② $\mathrm{- 6}$		③ $\mathrm{- 4}$
④ $\mathrm{- 2}$		⑤ $\mathrm{0}$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ②

[해설]
함수 $f (x)$가 $x = 1$에서 미분가능하므로 $x = 1$에서 연속이다.
$\lim_{x \rightarrow 1 -} f \left( x \right) = \lim_{x \rightarrow 1 +} f \left( x \right) = f \left( 1 \right)$
$\lim_{x \rightarrow 1 -} f \left( x \right) = \lim_{x \rightarrow 1 -} \left( 3 x + a \right) = a + 3$
$\lim_{x \rightarrow 1 +} f \left( x \right) = \lim_{x \rightarrow 1 +} \left( 2 x^{3} + b x + 1 \right) = b + 3$
$f \left( 1 \right) = a + 3$
$a + 3 = b + 3$, $a = b$
함수 $f (x)$가 $x = 1$에서 미분가능하므로
$\lim_{x \rightarrow 1 -} \frac{f \left( x \right) - f \left( 1 \right)}{x - 1} = \lim_{x \rightarrow 1 +} \frac{f \left( x \right) - f \left( 1 \right)}{x - 1}$
$\lim_{x \rightarrow 1 -} \frac{f \left( x \right) - f \left( 1 \right)}{x - 1} = \lim_{x \rightarrow 1 -} \frac{3 x + a - \left( a + 3 \right)}{x - 1} = 3$
$\lim_{x \rightarrow 1 +} \frac{f (x) - f (1)}{x - 1}$$= \lim_{x \rightarrow 1 +} \frac{\left( 2 x^{3} + a x + 1 \right) - (a + 3)}{x - 1}$
$= \lim_{x \rightarrow 1 +} \frac{(x - 1) \left( 2 x^{2} + 2 x + a + 2 \right)}{x - 1}$
$= a + 6$
$3 = a + 6$, $a = - 3$, $b = - 3$
따라서 $a + b = - 6$
