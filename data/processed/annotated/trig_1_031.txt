[문제]
$3 \tan \left( \pi + \theta \right) = 2 \sin \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right)$일 때, $\sin \theta$의 값은?
① $\frac{1}{6}$		② $\frac{1}{3}$		③ $\frac{1}{2}$
④ $\frac{2}{3}$		⑤ $\frac{5}{6}$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ③

[해설]
$3 \tan \left( \pi + \theta \right) = 2 \sin \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right)$에서
$3 \tan \theta = 2 \cos \theta$
$\frac{3 \sin \theta}{\cos \theta} = 2 \cos \theta$
$3 \sin \theta = 2 \cos^{2} \theta$
$3 \sin \theta = 2 - 2 \sin^{2} \theta$
$2 \sin^{2} \theta + 3 \sin \theta - 2 = \left( 2 \sin \theta - 1 \right) \left( \sin \theta + 2 \right) = 0$
$- 1 \le \sin \theta \le 1$이므로 $\sin \theta = \frac{1}{2}$
