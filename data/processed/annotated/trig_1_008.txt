[문제]
$\frac{3 \pi}{2} < \theta < 2 \pi$인 $\theta$에 대하여 $\cos^{2} \theta = \frac{1}{10}$일 때, $\tan \theta$의 값은?
① $- 3$		② $- 2$		③ $- 1$
④ $2$		⑤ $3$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ①

[해설]
$\frac{3 \pi}{2} < \theta < 2 \pi$이므로 $\cos^{2} \theta = \frac{1}{10}$에서
$\cos \theta = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$
$\sin^{2} \theta = 1 - \cos^{2} \theta = 1 - \frac{1}{10} = \frac{9}{10}$이므로
$\sin \theta = - \frac{3 \sqrt{10}}{10}$ $\left( \because \quad \frac{3 \pi}{2} < \theta < 2 \pi \right)$
$\therefore$ $\tan \theta = \frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} = \frac{- \frac{3 \sqrt{10}}{10}}{\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}} = - 3$
