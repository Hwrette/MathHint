[문제]
$\cos \theta = 4 \sin \theta$이고 $\sin \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right) < 0$일 때, $\cos \theta$의 값은?
① $- \frac{4 \sqrt{17}}{17}$	② $- \frac{\sqrt{17}}{17}$	③ $0$
④ $\frac{\sqrt{17}}{17}$	⑤ $\frac{4 \sqrt{17}}{17}$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ①

[해설]
$\sin^{2} \theta + \cos^{2} \theta = \left( \frac{\cos \theta}{4} \right)^{2} + \cos^{2} \theta = \frac{17}{16} \cos^{2} \theta = 1$
$\cos^{2} \theta = \frac{16}{17}$
$\sin \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right) = \cos \theta < 0$이므로 $\cos \theta = - \frac{4 \sqrt{17}}{17}$
