[문제]
$\sin \theta = - \frac{4}{5}$일 때, $\cos \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right)$의 값은?
① $- \frac{4}{5}$	② $- \frac{3}{5}$		③ $0$
④ $\frac{3}{5}$		⑤ $\frac{4}{5}$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ⑤

[해설]
$\cos \left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right) = - \sin \theta = \frac{4}{5}$
