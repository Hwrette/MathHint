[문제]
$\sin \theta = \frac{1}{3}$일 때, $\cos \left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$의 값은?
① $- \frac{7}{9}$	② $- \frac{2}{3}$		③ $- \frac{5}{9}$
④ $- \frac{4}{9}$	⑤ $- \frac{1}{3}$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ⑤

[해설]
$\cos \left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$$= - \sin \theta$$= - \frac{1}{3}$
