[문제]
다항함수 $f \left( x \right)$에 대하여 함수 $g \left( x \right)$를
$g \left( x \right) = \left( x^{2} + 2 x \right) f \left( x \right)$
라 하자. $f \left( 1 \right) = 2$, $f' \left( 1 \right) = 1$일 때, $g' \left( 1 \right)$의 값은?
① $5$		② $7$		③ $9$
④ $11$		⑤ $13$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ④

[해설]
$g' \left( x \right) = \left( 2 x + 2 \right) f \left( x \right) + \left( x^{2} + 2 x \right) f' \left( x \right)$
$g' \left( 1 \right) = 4 f \left( 1 \right) + 3 f' \left( 1 \right) = 4 \times 2 + 3 \times 1 = 11$
