[문제]
$\pi < \theta < \frac{3}{2} \pi$인 $\theta$에 대하여 $\tan \left( \pi - \theta \right) = - 2$일 때, $\cos \theta - \sin \theta$의 값은?
① $- \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$	② $- \frac{\sqrt{5}}{10}$	③ $0$
④ $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{10}$	⑤ $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$

[원본 정답·해설]
[정답] ⑤

[해설]
$\tan \left( \pi - \theta \right) = - \tan \theta = - 2$에서 $\tan \theta = 2$
$\frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} = 2$에서 $\sin \theta = 2 \cos \theta$
$\sin^{2} \theta + \cos^{2} \theta = 1$에서
$4 \cos^{2} \theta + \cos^{2} \theta = 1$, $\cos^{2} \theta = \frac{1}{5}$
$\pi < \theta < \frac{3}{2} \pi$이므로 $\cos \theta < 0$, $\sin \theta < 0$
$\cos \theta = - \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$이므로 $\sin \theta = 2 \cos \theta = - \frac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5}$
따라서 $\cos \theta - \sin \theta = - \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} - \left( - \frac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5} \right) = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
